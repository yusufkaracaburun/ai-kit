  - researches the audience, offer, live competitors, standards, and current inspiration before choosing colors
  - treats Refero Styles and galleries as inspiration rather than proof
  - produces three genuinely distinct directions using identical page content and journey for comparison
  - specifies semantic color roles, typography, composition, imagery, components, motion, strengths, risks, and cost
  - checks WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, responsive behavior, and reduced motion
  - avoids universal color-psychology claims and fixed-width body layouts
  - scores the directions, recommends one with confidence, and proposes lightweight user validation
  - does not implement a direction before the user selects it

# Prompt

We are launching a consumer website for young first-time buyers. Research the
right visual style, colors, and combinations before design starts. Show me
several homepage directions, use Refero and other useful design sites, and tell
me which direction you recommend. It must feel premium but approachable and
must convert well. Do not build it yet.
